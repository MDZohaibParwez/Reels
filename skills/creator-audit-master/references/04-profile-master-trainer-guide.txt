1. Profile Analysis - 7 Layers
• Layer 1 - First Impression: Username yaad rakhne layak? DP chhote me bhi saaf? Bio me 3 lines
  hai? Highlights + Grid saaf hai?
• Layer 2 - Niche Clarity: 10 sec me samajh aa raha hai page kis cheez ka hai? 80% ek niche, 20%
  experiment.
• Layer 3 - Hook / Content: 0-3 sec me rukne ki wajah hai? Curiosity / Shock / Problem / Result me se
  kuch hai?
• Layer 4 - Edit: Pacing, Beat pe Cut, Zoom, Subtitles, Color, Sound, Transitions - frame by frame check.
• Layer 5 - Caption + Hashtag + CTA: Pehli line hook hai? CTA hai? Hashtag sirf 4-5 niche wale?
• Layer 6 - Algorithm: Consistency, Posting Time, Engagement, Originality, Watch Time.
• Layer 7 - Audience: Viewer ko kya mila? Entertainment / Knowledge / Emotion? Save / Share karne ka
  mann kiya?


2. Log Kyu Nahi Dekh Rahe? Top 10 Reasons
• 1. Hook Weak Hai: Pehle 3 sec me kuch nahi hota, log scroll kar dete hai.
• 2. Slow Start: Intro, logo, black screen - sabse badi galti.
• 3. Quality Kharab: Blur, dark, awaaz saaf nahi.
• 4. Niche Clear Nahi: Algorithm ko samajh nahi aa raha kisko dikhaye.
• 5. Irregular Posting: 1 reel, phir 10 din gayab = reach reset.
• 6. Edit Over / Under: Har sec shake = cheap. Ek shot 15 sec = boring.
• 7. Music Voice Se Tez: Baat samajh hi nahi aayi.
• 8. Value Nahi Mili: Sirf dikhaya, sikhaya / hasaya / rulaya nahi.
• 9. CTA Nahi Hai: Log dekh ke chale gaye, follow nahi kiya.
• 10. Cover / Caption Weak: Bina play kiye click karne ka mann nahi.
AI ka kaam: Inme se tumhare case ke Top 3 reasons nikalna aur unhi pe fix dena.


3. Edit Improve Karne Ke 10 Golden Rules
• 1 Sec Rule: Har 1 sec me screen pe kuch naya - Cut / Zoom / Text / B-roll.
• Beat Pe Cut: Music ki dham pe cut lagao, random cut mat lagao.
• Bada Text: Subtitle itna bada ki bina awaaz ke bhi padha jaye. White + Black Outline, Bold.
• Zoom Ka Jadu: Important line pe 110-120% zoom-in. Retention badhta hai.
• 2 Transition Se Jyada Mat: Sirf Cut + Zoom + 1 Smooth. Baaki sab hatado.
• Color: Contrast +10, Saturation +5, Sharpen +15. Skin orange mat karo.
• Audio Balance: Voice 100%, Music 15-25%, SFX 30%. Music kabhi voice se upar nahi.
• Export: 1080x1920, 30fps, High Quality. Pehle gallery me save karo, direct mat bhejo.
• Loop: End aisa ki dobara dekhne ka mann kare. Last frame = First frame jaisa.
• First 3 Sec Me 3 Cheez: Bada Text + Tez Movement + Tez Sound - teeno ek saath.

5. Self-Fix - AI Khud Kya Banake Dega
• FIX 1 - Naya Bio: 3 options, 3-line formula me.
• FIX 2 - Naya Hook: Usi reel ke liye 3 naye hooks (Visual + Text + Voice).
• FIX 3 - Naya Caption + Hashtags: Ready-to-paste caption + 4-5 hashtags.
• FIX 4 - Edit Timeline Fix: 0-1 sec, 1-3 sec, 3-8 sec, 8-15 sec me kya karna hai.
• FIX 5 - Next 3 Reels Ke Ideas: Hook + Script + Music vibe ke saath.


6. Post Karne Se Pehle - 10 Point Checklist
Agar 8 se kam YES hai to post mat karo, pehle fix karo.
■ Kya pehle 3 sec me viewer rukega?
■ Kya bina awaaz ke bhi video samajh aa raha hai? (Subtitles)
■ Kya video 1080p saaf hai, blur to nahi?
■ Kya music voice se tez to nahi?
■ Kya caption ki pehli line hook hai?
■ Kya CTA hai? (Follow / Comment / Share)
■ Kya cover pe bada text hai?
■ Kya video 15-30 sec ke andar hai?
■ Kya ye reel mere niche se match karti hai?
■ Kya main khud is reel ko 2 baar dekhna chahunga?


7. 30 Din Ka Plan
Week 1 - Foundation: Bio + DP + Highlights fix. Roz 1 reel, sirf Hook pe focus.
Week 2 - Edit Master: Pacing + Subtitles + Color. Purani 3 reels ko re-edit karo.
Week 3 - Testing: 3 alag style try karo - 1 Story, 1 Value, 1 Emotional. Jo chala wahi pakdo.
Week 4 - Scale: Jo style chala uspe 5 reels. Collab + Trending audio + Strong CTA.
